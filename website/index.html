Ivanec i Ivančica — 630 Godina: Između Legende i Opstanka

2026 • Historical Documentary • Grad Ivanec • Režija: Timon Terzić

A cinematic odyssey through 630 years of Ivanec's written history — from volcanic origins and Maltese knights to torchlit sets, FPV flights, and the environmental defense of the mountain.

Požar u Varaždinu 1776

2026 • AI Concept Trailer • Grad Varaždin / TZ Varaždin • Režija: Timon Terzić

Commemorating the 250th anniversary of the Great Fire of Varaždin on April 25, 1776. Cinematic concept trailer using AI worldbuilding to visualize the flames and rebirth of Croatia's baroque capital.

Rimac Automobili — One-Take FPV Tour

2024 • FPV Drone Tour • Rimac Automobili • Režija: Timon Terzić

Single-take FPV drone flight through hypercar assembly lines, engineering labs, and production halls of Rimac Automobili in one continuous cinematic movement.

Advent u Varaždinu — Aftermovie

2024 • Festival Aftermovie • Turistička zajednica grada Varaždina • Režija: Timon Terzić

Official aftermovie for Advent u Varaždinu, capturing glowing baroque streets, the Advent Lift, and Croatia's celebrated winter festival.

100 Dvoraca Sjevera Hrvatske

2025 • Cinematic Short / Drama • Castle Network • Režija: Timon Terzić

A fairytale-structured short film following the romantic legends of northern Croatia's castles: Trakošćan, Veliki Tabor, Varaždin, and Čakovec.

Hrvatska Kovnica Novca — 30 Godina

2024 • Institutional Documentary • Hrvatska Kovnica Novca • Režija: Timon Terzić

Prestigious anniversary film celebrating 30 years of Croatia's national mint, featuring original score composition, narration, and custom CGI/VFX.

Build the Impossible — VUDRAG × Dubai

2025 • Cinematic Art Film • Nikola Vudrag • Režija: Timon Terzić

Cinematic exploration framing the raw forged iron of sculptor Nikola Vudrag against the architectural scale of Dubai.

Ni Traga — Irma (HRT DORA 2026)

2026 • Music Video • HRT DORA / Eurovision Selection • Režija: Timon Terzić

Atmospheric official music video for Irma's DORA 2026 Eurovision selection entry, built with a visual language of shadow, light, and performance.

The Studio
